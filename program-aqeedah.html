Aqeedah (Islamic Creed)

A careful study of Islamic belief: the six pillars of Iman, the names and attributes of Allah, and how to answer common doubts.

What the course covers

What students will learn

01

The Six Pillars of Iman

Belief in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day and Divine Decree.

02

Tawheed

The oneness of Allah in His Lordship, in worship, and in His names and attributes.

03

Answering Doubts

Calm, age-appropriate discussion of questions students hear at school or online, and how to respond to them.
