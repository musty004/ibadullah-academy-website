Safeguarding policy

How we keep children safe in our online classes, and what to do if you are ever worried.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers every teacher and member of staff at Ibadullah Online Academy, and every class we run. Most of our students are children, and keeping them safe matters more to us than anything else we do.

Our Safeguarding Lead

One person on our team is responsible for safeguarding. If you have a concern about a child's safety or about how a teacher has behaved, you can contact the Safeguarding Lead directly.

  • On WhatsApp: message us and ask for the Safeguarding Lead
  • By email: info@ibadullahacademy.com, with "Safeguarding" in the subject line

Every concern is taken seriously and handled in confidence.

Checking our teachers

Before a teacher is matched with any student, we check their identity and background. We also review our teachers regularly once they are teaching, and we run training so everyone knows what is expected of them.

How classes are run

  • Every class is one-to-one and takes place on Zoom, using the link we send to the parent or adult student.
  • Teachers keep their camera on, dress modestly, and teach from a quiet, tidy space.
  • For children under 12, we ask that a parent or guardian is at home and nearby during the class.
  • Parents are welcome to sit in on any lesson, at any age, without notice.
  • We do not record classes as standard. If a parent asks for a class to be recorded, we arrange it, share the recording only with that family, and delete it within 30 days.

Contact between teachers and children

All arrangements are made with parents, not with children.

  • Teachers do not contact children privately by phone, messaging apps or social media.
  • Teachers do not ask children for personal information, photos or contact details.
  • Homework, updates and changes to class times go to the parent.

If a teacher ever contacts your child directly, please tell us straight away.

Raising a concern

If something in a class worries you, or your child tells you something that worries you, contact the Safeguarding Lead as soon as you can. Here is what happens next:

  1. We reply within one hour, in sha Allah, and listen to what happened.
  2. We look into it carefully and in confidence. While we do, the teacher may be paused from teaching.
  3. We keep you informed and tell you what we have done.
  4. Where the law requires it, or a child may be at risk, we report it to the relevant authorities.

If a child is in immediate danger

Please contact the emergency services first, then let us know.

  • United Kingdom: 999
  • United States and Canada: 911
  • Australia: 000

In other countries, call your local emergency number or children's services.

What we ask of students

We ask students to be respectful to their teacher and to join classes from a suitable place. A teacher may end a class early if a student's behaviour is not appropriate, and we will talk to the parent about it.

Reviewing this policy

We review this policy at least once a year, and whenever something changes in how we teach.
