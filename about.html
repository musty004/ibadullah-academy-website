Our story

Why we started the academy

We started Ibadullah Online Academy in 2023 to connect Muslim families in the West with caring, qualified teachers who understand what it's like to raise children there.

We began with a small group of teachers helping a handful of families. Today we teach students in more than ten countries, and every class is still one-to-one.

  • Teachers are background and identity checked
  • Classes seven days a week, in your time zone
  • Male and female teachers
  • Parents can reach us on WhatsApp every day
  • Regular reviews and training for our teachers
Our promise

What we promise every family

1

If the trial doesn't feel right, you owe us nothing

There's no obligation after the free class. If the teacher isn't the right fit, we'll find another, or we'll part on good terms.

2

We'll be honest about your child's progress

We won't tell you your child is doing well just to keep you paying. You'll get honest feedback, because real progress is what matters.

3

We'll reply quickly

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within one hour, in sha Allah.

4

Money won't keep a family away

If a family genuinely can't afford our standard prices, we'll work something out together.

Safeguarding

Your child's safety comes first

Teachers are checked before they teach, classes are recorded only if you ask, and you're welcome to sit in on any lesson.

Checked teachers

Every teacher passes background and identity checks before teaching a student.

Recorded only if you ask

We don't record classes as standard. If you'd like a lesson recorded, just tell us.

You can always reach us

Message us about any concern, at any time, and we'll reply within one hour, in sha Allah.
