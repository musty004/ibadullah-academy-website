How it works

Starting takes four simple steps

You'll always know what happens next.

Step 1

Fill in the form

Tell us the student's age, level and the subject you're interested in. It takes about three minutes.

Step 2

We match a teacher

We reply within one hour, in sha Allah, and match your child with a suitable teacher, usually within a day.

Step 3

Free trial class

Your child meets the teacher, has their level checked and gets a feel for the classes. There's nothing to pay.

Step 4

Choose your plan

If you're happy, pick a plan and a weekly time, and classes begin.
